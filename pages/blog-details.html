5 Signs Your Brakes Need Immediate Replacement

Your vehicle's braking system is arguably its most critical safety component. Ignoring warning signs can lead to compromised safety and more expensive repairs down the line.

1. Squeaking or Squealing Noises

One of the most common early warning signs is a high-pitched squealing noise when you apply the brakes. This is often caused by a small metal indicator built into brake pads specifically to warn you when they are getting thin.
